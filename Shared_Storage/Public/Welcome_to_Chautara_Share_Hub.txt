Features:
- 100% Offline: Works within your school Wi-Fi, LAN, or Hotspot without internet.
- Cross-Device: Open the link or scan the QR code from any smartphone, tablet, or PC.
- High-Speed Upload & Download: Drag and drop files to share them across devices.
- Drive Sharing: Plug in a USB flash drive and share it across the school network.
- Private Vault: Admin password-protected storage for confidential school documents.
